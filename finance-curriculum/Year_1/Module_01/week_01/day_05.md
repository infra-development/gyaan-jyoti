# Big Question

> **How did people meet their needs before money existed?**

Today, we will answer the same question we asked at the beginning of the week.

---

# Lesson Theme

This week, you discovered that people have needs, different skills, and different jobs.

You learned that people make different goods and provide different services.

Finally, you discovered that people began exchanging these goods and services through trade.

Today, we will connect all these ideas together.

# Learning Objectives

By the end of today's lesson, the student will be able to:

* Explain why people depend on one another.
* Distinguish between needs and wants.
* Identify goods and services.
* Explain why specialization leads to trade.
* Describe how people met their needs before money existed.

# Story Time

## The Village Before Money

A long time ago, there was a peaceful village.

The farmer grew crops.

The carpenter built tables and chairs.

The tailor stitched clothes.

The doctor treated sick people.

The teacher taught children.

The baker baked fresh bread every morning.

Nobody could make everything alone.

So they exchanged what they had.

The farmer received clothes.

The tailor received vegetables.

The doctor received bread.

The baker received furniture.

Every family had what they needed because everyone shared their skills.

There were no supermarkets.

There were no banks.

There was no money.

Yet the village worked together because everyone understood one simple idea.

> **When people help one another, everyone benefits.**

# Parent Notes (For Adults)

The goal was **not** to teach definitions but to develop curiosity about how society works.
