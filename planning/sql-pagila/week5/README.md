# Week 1

## Theme

SQL Foundations & Business Thinking

### Goal

Understand relational databases and answer simple business questions.

### Major Topics

* Database Exploration
* SELECT
* DISTINCT
* WHERE
* ORDER BY
* LIMIT
* GROUP BY
* HAVING
* Aggregate Functions
* INNER JOIN
* LEFT JOIN
* NULL

### Business Focus

Understanding business data.

### Outcome

Students can answer straightforward business questions using SQL.

---

# Week 2

## Theme

Advanced Joins & Multi-table Reporting

### Goal

Become comfortable navigating relationships across multiple tables.

### Major Topics

* INNER JOIN
* LEFT JOIN
* Multi-table JOINs
* Relationship Traversal
* Relationship Paths
* Bridge Tables
* Duplicate Thinking
* COUNT(DISTINCT)
* Business Reporting
* Finding Missing Data
* Operational Reporting

### Business Focus

Operational reporting.

### Outcome

Students can confidently navigate complex schemas, understand business relationships, and build operational reports using multiple related tables.

---

# Week 3

## Theme

Data Transformation, Conditional Logic & Advanced JOINs

### Goal

Prepare raw business data for reporting while learning the remaining JOIN concepts.

### Major Topics

### Advanced JOIN Concepts

* SELF JOIN
* USING
* RIGHT JOIN (Overview)
* FULL JOIN (Overview)

### Conditional Logic

* CASE
* COALESCE
* NULLIF

### Data Conversion

* CAST
* Type Conversion

### String Functions

* CONCAT
* UPPER
* LOWER
* TRIM
* REPLACE
* SUBSTRING
* LENGTH

### Date & Time Functions

* CURRENT_DATE
* CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
* AGE
* EXTRACT
* DATE_TRUNC
* TO_CHAR

### Business Focus

Preparing business-ready data.

### Outcome

Students can clean, transform, and format business data while understanding all commonly used JOIN techniques.
