# Week 2 Final Business Challenge
## Your Second Week as a Data Analyst at Pagila DVD Rentals

---

# Welcome

Congratulations!

You've completed your second week as a Junior Data Analyst at **Pagila DVD Rentals**.

Your manager smiles and says:

> "You've learned how our database is connected.
>
> Now it's time to answer real business questions.
>
> Every department needs reports before tomorrow morning.
>
> Don't start writing SQL immediately.
>
> First understand the business.
>
> Then identify the relationship path.
>
> Finally build the report."

This challenge combines everything you've learned during Week 2.

---

# Rules

For every request complete the following steps.

## Step 1

Identify the Business Objective.

Examples:

- Retrieve information
- Compare performance
- Measure activity
- Find missing information
- Analyze business processes

---

## Step 2

Identify the Business Entities.

Examples:

- Customer
- Rental
- Inventory
- Film
- Category
- Actor
- Store
- Payment
- City
- Country

---

## Step 3

Draw the Relationship Path.

Example

```
Customer

↓

Rental

↓

Inventory

↓

Film
```

---

## Step 4

Identify the Thinking Pattern.

Examples

- Relationship Path
- Business Reporting
- Missing Information
- Duplicate Thinking
- Business Process
- Performance Comparison

---

## Step 5

Identify the SQL Concepts.

Examples

- INNER JOIN
- LEFT JOIN
- GROUP BY
- HAVING
- DISTINCT
- COUNT(DISTINCT)
- ORDER BY
- LIMIT

---

## Step 6

Write the SQL.

# Final Thought

Week 1 taught you individual SQL building blocks.

Week 2 taught you how those building blocks work together.

Professional SQL developers don't memorize queries.

They understand how businesses work, how data is related, and how to navigate those relationships.

That ability is what transforms SQL from a programming language into a business analysis tool.
